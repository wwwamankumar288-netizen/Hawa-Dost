WHO AIR QUALITY GUIDELINES — KEY FACTS FOR HAWA DOST

ANNUAL MEAN LIMITS (WHO 2021):
- PM2.5: 5 µg/m³ (annual mean) | 15 µg/m³ (24-hour mean)
- PM10: 15 µg/m³ (annual mean) | 45 µg/m³ (24-hour mean)
- NO2: 10 µg/m³ (annual mean) | 25 µg/m³ (24-hour mean)
- O3: 60 µg/m³ (peak season) | 100 µg/m³ (8-hour mean)
- CO: 4 mg/m³ (24-hour mean)

INDIA CONTEXT (CPCB 2023 data):
- Delhi average PM2.5: 102 µg/m³ (20x WHO limit)
- Winter peak (Nov-Jan) often 300-500 µg/m³
- Punjab stubble burning contributes 30-40% of Delhi's winter smog
- Vehicle emissions: 40% of urban PM2.5 in metro cities
- Industries: 20% of urban PM2.5
- Construction dust: 10% of urban PM2.5

CIGARETTE EQUIVALENT (Berkeley Earth formula):
- Every 22 µg/m³ of PM2.5 = smoking approximately 1 cigarette per day
- Delhi winter average = equivalent to smoking 4-5 cigarettes/day
- Peak smog days (PM2.5 > 400 µg/m³) = 18+ cigarettes/day

HEALTH EFFECTS BY EXPOSURE DURATION:
Short-term (hours): Eye irritation, throat irritation, coughing, reduced lung function
Medium-term (days): Increased asthma attacks, bronchitis, cardiovascular stress
Long-term (years): Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), lung cancer, heart disease, reduced life expectancy (average 5+ years in Delhi)

VULNERABLE GROUPS (highest risk):
1. Children under 12 — developing lungs, faster breathing rate = more exposure
2. Elderly (65+) — reduced lung function, pre-existing conditions
3. Pregnant women — exposure linked to low birth weight, preterm birth
4. People with asthma/COPD/heart disease
5. Outdoor workers (construction, traffic police, vendors)

AQI TO PM2.5 CONVERSION (CPCB India):
AQI 0-50 = PM2.5 0-30 µg/m³ — Good
AQI 51-100 = PM2.5 31-60 µg/m³ — Satisfactory
AQI 101-200 = PM2.5 61-90 µg/m³ — Moderate
AQI 201-300 = PM2.5 91-120 µg/m³ — Poor
AQI 301-400 = PM2.5 121-250 µg/m³ — Very Poor
AQI 401-500 = PM2.5 >250 µg/m³ — Severe

MASK EFFECTIVENESS:
- N95/N99 respirators: Block 95-99% of PM2.5 when properly fitted
- Surgical masks: Block only 20-50% of PM2.5
- Cloth masks: Minimal protection against PM2.5
- Always ensure no gaps around cheeks, chin, nose bridge
- Masks must be replaced every 8 hours or when damp
