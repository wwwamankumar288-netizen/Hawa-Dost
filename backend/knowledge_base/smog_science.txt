SMOG SCIENCE — HOW WINTER AIR POLLUTION WORKS

=== WHAT IS SMOG? ===

Smog (smoke + fog) is a type of air pollution formed when sunlight reacts with pollutants from vehicles, factories, burning, and dust.

TWO TYPES:
1. Photochemical smog: Ozone (O3) + NO2 formed by sunlight + vehicle emissions. Worse in hot weather.
2. Industrial/Sulfurous smog: SO2 + particulates from coal burning. Classic London-type. Delhi winter smog is a MIX of both.

DELHI WINTER SMOG RECIPE:
- Low temperatures (8-15°C) + High humidity (70-90%)
- Temperature inversion: Warm air layer traps cold, polluted air near ground
- Multiple emission sources: Vehicles, power plants, construction, Diwali firecrackers, stubble burning
- Weak winds prevent dispersion
- Result: PM2.5 can reach 500-900 µg/m³ — 100x WHO limit

TEMPERATURE INVERSION EXPLAINED:
Normally: Warm air near ground rises, dispersing pollutants upward
Inversion: Cold air at ground, warmer air above = pollutants CANNOT rise
Like putting a lid on a pot — smog gets trapped at ground level
Inversions worst: Early morning (5-9 AM), on calm, clear nights

SEASONAL PATTERN (Delhi/Indo-Gangetic Plain):
- October-November: Stubble burning peak + post-monsoon dryness
- December-January: Peak smog season, lowest wind speeds
- February: Gradual improvement as temperatures rise
- March-September: Better air quality (monsoon washes PM)

PARTICULATE MATTER SIZES:
- PM10 (coarse): Diameter < 10 micrometres. Settles in nose/throat. Causes irritation.
- PM2.5 (fine): Diameter < 2.5 µm. Penetrates deep into lungs. MOST DANGEROUS.
- PM1 (ultrafine): < 1 µm. Can enter bloodstream through lung walls.
- Black carbon (BC): Tiny combustion particles. Strong global warming agent + carcinogen.

HOW PM2.5 HARMS THE BODY:
Path: Nose → Bronchi → Alveoli (air sacs) → Bloodstream
Effects:
- Inflammation of airways within hours of exposure
- Oxidative stress damages cell DNA
- Cardiovascular: Increases blood pressure, clotting risk
- Neural: PM2.5 linked to dementia and cognitive decline
- Reproductive: Reduces male sperm quality
- In children: Stunts lung development permanently

HOW SEASONS AFFECT POLLUTION IN INDIA:
Pre-monsoon (Mar-May): Rising temperatures lift inversions. Better air. Dust storms from Rajasthan add PM10.
Monsoon (Jun-Sep): Rain washes particles. Best air quality of year.
Post-monsoon (Oct-Nov): Sudden temperature drop + wind shift + stubble burning = worst pollution onset.
Winter (Dec-Feb): Cold, calm, humid = worst smog conditions. Inversions daily.

CONTRIBUTION SOURCES TO DELHI PM2.5 (IIT Delhi SAFAR data):
- Stubble burning (Nov): 30-40% of PM2.5
- Vehicles: 28%
- Road and construction dust: 24%
- Industries: 11%
- Domestic burning (cooking, heating): 6%
- Other: 1%

WHY MORNING IS WORST:
- Overnight inversion is strongest at dawn (5-7 AM)
- Exhaust from early traffic + no wind = concentrated pollution
- School run time = children's highest exposure window
- Recommendation: Keep children indoors until 10 AM on high-AQI days

WHY FIREWORKS ARE SO HARMFUL:
- Diwali firecrackers spike PM2.5 to 1000-2000 µg/m³ in hours
- Contain heavy metals: barium, lead, antimony, copper — toxic even in tiny amounts
- PM2.5 from fireworks stays airborne for 24-48 hours in calm conditions
- One night of fireworks can take 7-10 days for air to recover

GREENHOUSE EFFECT OF BLACK CARBON:
- Black carbon from biomass burning warms atmosphere 1500x more than CO2 per gram
- Settles on Himalayan glaciers, accelerating glacier melt (water security threat)
- 2-week atmospheric lifetime vs CO2's 100+ years — cutting BC has IMMEDIATE climate benefit
