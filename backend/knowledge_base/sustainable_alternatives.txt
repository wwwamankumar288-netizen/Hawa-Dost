SUSTAINABLE ALTERNATIVES FOR SOUTH ASIAN URBAN FAMILIES

=== TRANSPORT ALTERNATIVES ===

1. PERSONAL CAR (Diesel/Petrol) → ALTERNATIVES

Electric Vehicle (EV):
- CO2 savings: 2.1 tonnes/year for average Indian commuter (12km daily)
- Cost: Higher upfront (Tata Nexon EV ₹14-17L), but ₹15,000/year fuel savings
- Charging: Home charging (₹1-2/km vs ₹7-9/km for petrol)
- Air quality benefit: Zero tailpipe PM2.5 emissions
- Government incentives: FAME II subsidy up to ₹1.5L on 2-wheelers, state subsidies

Metro/Public Transport:
- CO2 savings: 80% reduction vs private car
- Cost: ₹500-2000/month vs ₹5000-15000 for car fuel + parking
- Delhi Metro: 65km/hr average speed vs 20km/hr car in traffic
- Health: Avoid road pollution absorption (car cabin has 2x roadside pollution levels)

E-Rickshaw/E-Auto:
- CO2 savings: 95% vs auto rickshaw
- Cost: ₹8-15 per km (similar to petrol auto)
- Availability: 1.5 million e-rickshaws in India, growing 40% annually
- Good for: Last-mile connectivity from metro

Cycling (for <8 km commute):
- CO2 savings: 1.5 tonnes/year
- Health benefit: 30 min cycling = 50% lung health improvement (with N95 mask)
- Risk: Use cycling mask (N95), avoid peak traffic hours
- Best hours: 10 AM - 4 PM (lower pollution)

2. TWO-WHEELER (Petrol) → ALTERNATIVES

Electric Scooter:
- Options: Ola S1 Pro, Ather 450X, Bajaj Chetak Electric
- Cost: ₹1-1.5L (similar to good petrol scooter)
- Savings: ₹30,000-50,000/year on fuel
- Range: 100-150km per charge
- Subsidies: Up to ₹68,000 under FAME II + state subsidy

BS6 Upgraded Petrol Scooter:
- 30-50% less PM emissions vs older models
- If replacing >8 year old vehicle: immediate 60% pollution reduction

=== COOKING ALTERNATIVES ===

3. BIOMASS/WOOD COOKING → ALTERNATIVES

Improved Biomass Cookstove (Chulha):
- PM2.5 reduction: 50-70% vs traditional open fire
- Cost: ₹1,500-3,000 (govt subsidized under PMUY)
- Available: Block level agricultural offices

LPG (Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana):
- Free connection for BPL families under PMUY
- PM2.5 reduction: 95% vs biomass
- Monthly cylinder cost: ₹800-1,000 (subsidized ₹200-300)
- Respiratory disease reduction: 30-40% in household members

Electric Induction Cooker:
- Zero indoor emissions
- Cost: ₹1,500-4,000 (single burner to 2-burner)
- Electricity: ₹2-5 per cooking session
- Best with: Green electricity or solar panels

4. GENERATOR (Diesel) → ALTERNATIVES

Solar Home System:
- 1kW system: Powers fan, lights, phone charging, small appliances
- Cost: ₹60,000-80,000 installed (10-year payback)
- Monthly savings: ₹1,500-2,500 on diesel + electricity
- Subsidy: PM Surya Ghar Yojana — 60% subsidy on 1-3 kW systems (up to ₹78,000)
- Payback: 3-5 years with subsidy
- Benefit: Zero noise, zero PM emissions, 25-year lifespan

Lithium-ion UPS/Inverter:
- Cleaner than lead-acid batteries
- Charges from grid during off-peak hours
- Cost: ₹25,000-40,000 for 1kW capacity

=== HEATING ALTERNATIVES ===

5. COAL/WOOD ROOM HEATERS → ALTERNATIVES

Electric Room Heater:
- Zero indoor PM emissions (all pollution at power plant, away from home)
- Cost: ₹1,500-5,000 (oil-filled radiator best — no dry air effect)
- Energy efficient: Oil-filled radiators maintain heat when switched off

Reverse-Cycle Air Conditioner (Heat Pump):
- 300% more energy efficient than resistance heating
- Doubles as AC in summer
- Cost: ₹25,000-40,000
- Running cost: 60% lower than electric heater

Blanket + Thermal Clothing:
- Wool blankets, fleece, thermal innerwear eliminate heating need in mild cold
- Best for: AQI management + avoiding combustion indoors
- Cost: ₹500-3,000, one-time purchase

=== AGRICULTURAL ALTERNATIVES (STUBBLE BURNING) ===

6. CROP RESIDUE BURNING → ALTERNATIVES (Punjab/Haryana farmers)

Happy Seeder Machine:
- Sows wheat directly into paddy stubble without burning
- Subsidy: 50-80% from state governments
- Saves: ₹5,000-8,000/acre (no burning cost)
- Benefit: Improves soil organic matter, retains moisture

Baling + Selling to Industries:
- Stubble sold to thermal power plants, paper mills, packaging industry
- Price: ₹2-3 per kg, 5-8 tonnes/acre = ₹10,000-24,000 per acre additional income
- Contact: Punjab Mandi Board for authorized aggregators

Biochar Production:
- Convert stubble to biochar (charcoal-like soil amendment)
- Biochar sells for ₹15-25/kg
- Improves soil carbon, reduces need for fertilizer

=== COMMUNITY ALTERNATIVES ===

7. INDIVIDUAL DIESEL GENERATOR COLONIES → COMMUNITY SOLAR MICROGRID
- 20-50 households: shared 50kW solar installation
- Cost per household: ₹15,000-25,000 (vs ₹8,000/year on diesel)
- Zero air emissions from generators
- Contact: MNRE (Ministry of New & Renewable Energy) for group subsidy schemes
