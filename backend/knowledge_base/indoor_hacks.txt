INDOOR AIR QUALITY HACKS FOR SOUTH ASIAN HOMES

=== FREE HACKS (No Cost) ===

1. STRATEGIC WINDOW MANAGEMENT
- Keep all windows CLOSED during peak smog hours: 6-10 AM and 6-10 PM
- Open windows briefly (5-10 min) only between 11 AM - 3 PM when sun disperses smog
- Use wet cloth strips around door gaps during severe smog days (AQI > 300)
- Check for gaps under doors and seal with rolled towels

2. WET MOPPING OVER DRY SWEEPING
- Never dry sweep floors during smog season — it resuspends settled particles
- Always wet mop floors daily with a damp mop
- Use damp cloth for dusting surfaces — dry dusting sends PM back into air
- Change mop water frequently; dirty water recontaminates floors

3. VENTILATION DURING COOKING
- Always turn on exhaust fan before lighting stove, not after
- Keep kitchen window open during and 15 minutes after cooking
- Avoid high-heat cooking (deep frying, tandoor) on severe smog days
- Use lids on pots to reduce steam and particulate dispersion

4. SHOE-FREE HOME RULE
- Remove shoes at entrance — outdoor PM2.5, lead, pesticides enter on shoe soles
- Place a doormat outside AND inside entrance
- Wipe pets' paws before they enter from outdoors

5. INCENSE AND CANDLES — MINIMIZE
- Incense sticks (agarbatti) emit PM2.5 at 45x the WHO limit per hour
- Scented candles release benzene and toluene — known carcinogens
- Replace with diffusers or natural floral arrangements

6. HUMIDITY CONTROL (Free)
- PM2.5 concentration increases in dry air
- Hang wet towels in rooms to increase humidity to 40-60%
- Place bowls of water near heaters in winter
- Do NOT over-humidify (>70%) — promotes mold growth

=== LOW COST HACKS (Under ₹500) ===

7. DIY BOX FAN AIR PURIFIER (₹0-300 for filter)
- Attach a MERV-13 or HEPA furnace filter to back of a box fan with tape
- Tape filter securely to intake side of fan (the flat back)
- Filter cost: ₹200-500, replaceable monthly
- Effectiveness: Reduces PM2.5 by 60-80% in a single room
- Run for 20-30 minutes to clean a 200 sq ft room

8. CHARCOAL BAG AIR FRESHENER/PURIFIER
- Activated charcoal absorbs VOCs, formaldehyde, and odors
- Place 200g activated charcoal sachets (₹150-200 on Amazon) in each room
- Replace every 2-3 months (recharge by placing in sun for 2 hours monthly)

9. SALT LAMP (₹300-800)
- Himalayan salt lamps emit negative ions that attract PM2.5
- Best in bedroom for overnight air purification
- Effectiveness lower than HEPA but measurable improvement in small spaces

10. BEESWAX CANDLES (₹200-400)
- Unlike paraffin, beeswax candles emit negative ions and minimal pollutants
- Actually improve indoor air slightly when burned in ventilated room

=== MEDIUM COST (₹500-2000) ===

11. PORTABLE HEPA AIR PURIFIER
- Look for CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) matching room size: CADR 100 for ~200 sq ft
- Brands: Dyson (expensive), Xiaomi Mi Air Purifier 3 (₹8,000 — worth it), 
  Kent Aura (₹4,500), Honeywell Air Touch (₹5,000-8,000)
- Always check for true HEPA H13 certification
- Run continuously on auto mode; replace filters every 6-12 months

12. AIR QUALITY MONITOR
- Real-time PM2.5 reading helps you know when to close windows or run purifier
- Xiaomi Mi Air Monitor (₹2,500) — most affordable with app connectivity
- Readings above 35 µg/m³ indoors = run purifier immediately

=== AIR-PURIFYING PLANTS FOR INDIAN HOMES ===

TOP PLANTS BY PM2.5 ABSORPTION (CSIR research):
1. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum / Pankhuri) — absorbs formaldehyde, CO
2. Snake Plant (Sansevieria / Maa ki Zubaan) — releases O2 at night, absorbs benzene
3. Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum) — absorbs mold spores, benzene, trichloroethylene  
4. Aloe Vera — absorbs benzene and formaldehyde from cleaning products
5. Bamboo Palm — excellent for removing formaldehyde
6. Indian Basil (Tulsi) — traditional air purifier, also mosquito repellent
7. Golden Pothos (Money Plant) — extremely hardy, absorbs xylene and benzene
8. Rubber Plant (Ficus elastica) — absorbs airborne chemicals efficiently
9. Areca Palm — natural humidifier + air purifier combination
10. Dracaena — removes trichloroethylene from air

PLACEMENT GUIDE:
- Bedroom: Snake plant (releases O2 at night), Aloe vera
- Kitchen: Aloe vera, Golden Pothos (absorbs cooking fumes)
- Living room: Areca Palm, Peace Lily, Spider Plant
- Study/Office: Snake Plant, Dracaena
- Near entrance: Bamboo Palm (large, traps outdoor particles)

For maximum effect: 2 medium plants (6-inch pot) per 100 sq ft
